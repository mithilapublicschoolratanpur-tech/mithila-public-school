About Our School

Mithila Public School, Ratanpur was established in 1997. We provide quality education from Nursery to Class VIII. Our aim is to develop disciplined, confident and responsible students.

Our Facilities

📚 Library

A well-stocked library with books, magazines and study materials to develop reading habits and knowledge.

💻 Computer Lab

Modern computer education with practical learning to improve digital skills and technology awareness.

🏫 Smart Class

Interactive teaching using digital content, presentations and audio-visual learning methods.

⚽ Sports & Games

Indoor and outdoor sports activities that promote physical fitness, teamwork and discipline.

🚌 Safe Transport

Safe and comfortable transportation facility with trained drivers for students' convenience.

🏡 Hostel Facility

Clean, secure and comfortable hostel accommodation with proper care and supervision.
